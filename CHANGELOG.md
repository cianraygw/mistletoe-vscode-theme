# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Christmas countdown in the status bar, with a Christmas Eve option.
- Rotating festive messages in the status bar (typewriter or static), with custom messages.
- New Christmas Countdown view: live clock, falling snow, advent calendar lights and a scrolling message banner.
- All animations respect VS Code's Reduce Motion setting.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release: Mistletoe Midnight (dark) and Mistletoe Frost (light).
